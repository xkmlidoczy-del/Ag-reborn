# V9 sound source provenance

The 288 in-game mono Vorbis files are edited and layered mixes of original synthesis and selected CC0 foley/creature recordings. Rebuild with `tools/build_v9_audio.py`; source file URLs, pinned mirror commits, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`.

- **rubberduck**, “80 CC0 creature SFX”, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx . The files are fetched from the pinned `lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds` GitHub mirror indicated in the manifest. The creator describes most voices as self-performed and filtered.
- **Kenney**, “Impact Sounds”, CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds .
- **Kenney**, “RPG Audio”, CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio .
- **Kenney**, “Sci-fi Sounds”, CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds .

Kenney source files and their included license notices are fetched from the pinned `GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat` GitHub mirror indicated in the manifest. Original recordings have been trimmed, pitch-shifted, filtered and mixed with synthesized audio. CC0 license information: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Credit is retained for traceability even where not required.
